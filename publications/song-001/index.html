SONG 001 · Worship

The Word Is Alive

Read the original worship lyrics and arrangement concept. A recording has not yet been produced.

Worship lyrics

Intro: Spoken

Many see only pages. Many hear only words.
But this is no ordinary book.
For within these sacred Scriptures lies a revelation that transforms the human heart.
Holy Spirit, open our eyes.

Verse 1

Some see a book of ancient stories,
Words preserved from days gone by.
But I have found a hidden treasure,
Where Your truth and glory shine.

Every chapter holds a wonder,
Every promise speaks of You.
And the deeper I go into Your presence,
The more Your light comes shining through.

Pre-Chorus

Open my eyes, O Holy Spirit,
Take me deeper into truth.
Not just knowledge in my mind, Lord,
I want my heart transformed by You.

Chorus

Your Word is alive! Your Word is the light!
Shining through the darkness,
Turning night into day!
More than the letters, more than the pages,
Your Spirit reveals the glory of Your ways!
Don't just change what I know,
Change who I become!
Let Your living Word make me like Your Son!
Oh, Your Word is alive in me!

Verse 2

I have searched the sacred Scriptures,
Found a fountain without end.
Every truth reveals Your wisdom,
Every word draws me again.

Not to read and walk away, Lord,
Not to gather knowledge alone.
But to know the One who spoke creation,
And to make Your presence home.

Pre-Chorus

Beyond the ink, beyond the pages,
Let Your Spirit make me see.
Every word that You have spoken
Has the power to transform me.

Chorus

Your Word is alive! Your Word is the light!
Shining through the darkness,
Turning night into day!
More than the letters, more than the pages,
Your Spirit reveals the glory of Your ways!
Don't just change what I know,
Change who I become!
Let Your living Word make me like Your Son!
Oh, Your Word is alive in me!

Bridge

Open my eyes! Open my heart!
Reveal the mysteries of who You are!
Take me deeper! Take me higher!
Let Your Word become my fire!
Not information, but transformation!
Not empty religion, but divine revelation!
Let me see Jesus! Let me know Jesus!
Let every word draw me closer to Jesus!

Final Chorus

Your Word is alive! Your Word is the light!
The heavens declare it, Your Spirit reveals it!
Not just what I know, but who I become!
Let Your Word transform me till I look like Your Son!
Hallelujah! Your Word is alive!

Outro

Your Word is alive in me...
Open my eyes to see...
And change who I become...
Amen.
